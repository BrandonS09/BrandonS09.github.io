I build systems that work across the full stack, from mechanical design and electrical integration to software and computer vision.

I am currently pursuing research collaborations in robotics and human-computer interaction while developing independent engineering projects. Previously, I designed and built the modular motorized test track at [Verdant Robotics](https://www.verdantrobotics.com/).

I have also contributed to open source projects including [Mattermost](https://mattermost.com/) and [WPILib](https://wpilib.org/).

My research on quantum neural network architectures is currently under peer review for publication.

Outside of engineering, I photograph motorsport, most recently at the Cold Start 2026 in San Francisco.
